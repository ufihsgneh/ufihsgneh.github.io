Clearly state the **research questions** and the target group of users; 

Draft an **experiment plan**, explain your research rationale and identify possible evaluation methods. You will need to consider what sampling methods to use, and what types of data to collect, etc.;

Prepare necessary materials for your proposed evaluation methods, such as **survey scripts** and **interview questions**. You will also need to prepare an **information sheet** and **consent form** for participants; 

Implement your research design and try to collect some **sample data**; 

Present your work to the general audience and reflect on your study implementation
