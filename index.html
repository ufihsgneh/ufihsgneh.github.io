1 Background & Problem

  • Background information:As a new PG student of AIAT, we had a mission to find a supervisor in our Academy. We found that it wastes a lot of time to find a supervisor in school official website, so we create an agent for students who also face this problem.
  • Hypothesis: The AI agent is more effective than the official school website for finding supervisors.

3 Study Design

Experiment flow and procedure:

IV: with or without the agent
DV: Completion Time & Satisfaction Score
Task: You are a senior undergraduate student or a second-year master's student, and you are about to choose an advisor for the career direction or academic direction you plan to pursue in the future. To do this, you need to know which teachers' research directions match yours, and even understand whether their project experience and subfields match yours. (For example, you want to find an advisor in Artificial Intelligence — Natural Language Processing — Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning, or Artificial Intelligence — Computer Vision — Object Detection.) Example: I need to find teachers working in VR-related fields. Please provide me with the advisors' contact information and research directions, and give me further guidance on specific directions.
Procedure: 1.Informed Consent and Introduction: Welcome participants, introduce the purpose of the study, obtain informed consent, and ask them to complete a background questionnaire. 2.Training/Practice Session: Allow participants to become familiar with the basic operations of the AI Agent and ensure they understand the task requirements. 3.Formal Experiment: Clearly state the task order (and whether randomization was used to counterbalance order effects), as well as any time limits for each task. 4.Data Collection: Explain how data were collected while participants performed the tasks (e.g., automatically recorded task completion time via system logs, screen recording, observer-recorded errors). 5.Post-task Questionnaire and Interview: After the tasks, ask participants to complete questionnaires such as the SUS, NASA-TLX, or an experience questionnaire, and conduct brief semi-structured interviews to understand the reasons behind the quantitative data. 6.Acknowledgments and Compensation: Thank participants and provide compensation.

4 Data Analysis & Results

Core Conclusion: The completion time using the AI agent (Time_agent) is significantly lower than the official search (Time_official), and the difference is statistically significant (p < .05). However, the Official Website still scores higher in providing confidence.

5 Findings & Conclusion

  • AI Agent significantly reduces the time to find supervisors compared to the official website.
  • Users are highly satisfied with the Agent's efficiency, but trust remains a factor.

6 Recommendation

  • For Agent Enhance data accuracy to increase user confidence.
  • For Students: Use the AI Agent for quick initial screening of supervisors.
  • For School: Students have great confidence on the official website. School can consider integrating AI search into the official website.
